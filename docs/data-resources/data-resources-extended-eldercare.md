## At a glance: projects with data support

Compared to the two existing resource lists, this article includes 13 newly added resources or data series, numbered 1–13. Items 1–8 and 11, totaling 9 items, have found evidence for public access and reuse; OECD data for 12 is publicly accessible, but third-party rights must be verified before using specific country slices; 9, 10, 13 respectively involve scheduling record privacy review or research applications. Here, "usable" means there is justification for entering the project preparation phase, but does not mean the model has been downloaded, cleaned, or validated.

Compared to existing lists, the new resources can more directly support three topics: using the Nordic official summary table to examine regional differences in care services; BRFSS was used to analyze how self-reported cognitive decliners received help and discussed issues with healthcare workers; BRFSS was used to analyze the distribution of home care time and tasks. Additionally, it can perform digital service coverage blind spot checks and EEG model data leak audits. Scheduling optimizes public sources for matching existing fields, but privacy checks cannot be skipped. There is still insufficient data to support future dementia risk prediction, automated referrals, or treatment recommendations for individuals in the Nordic region.

This supplement advances Demo Day's sections on "Issues and Target Users," "Data Sources, Permissions and Evidence," "Success Metrics," and "Limitations and Security," mainly corresponding to the "Impact and Significance" and "Evidence and Responsible Use" scores.
