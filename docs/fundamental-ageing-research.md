# Fundamental ageing research: a funding comparison worth testing

Research note, 11 September 2026. This advances novelty, policy relevance and the Demo Day evidence discussion. The two numerical records below are supplemental candidates awaiting human verification; they are withheld from the public evidence release.

The proposed question is: how much public money supports research into the biological mechanisms of ageing, by country and fiscal year? A national total needs a defined perimeter before it can support a ranking. Do not substitute eldercare expenditure, dementia funding, all health research, or a broad longevity programme.

The SQLite `funding_observations` table stores amount, currency, fiscal period, financial stage, programme scope and an explicit `is_national_fundamental_total` flag. Both current numerical candidates have that flag set to false. Their source fingerprints and review versions use the same evidence pipeline as the policy records.

A defensible next dataset would classify public grant records by their stated scientific objective, using biological mechanisms of ageing as the inclusion rule. Record project identifiers, funder, award period, annual disbursements where available, research type, direct/indirect cost treatment, domestic/overseas recipients and the supporting abstract passage. Deduplicate cross-funder records; do not sum an award and its subawards. Keep ambiguous projects in a separate queue.

Two reviewers should label a held-out grant sample before publishing totals. Publish agreement, errors and sensitivity to inclusion rules. Treat total awards, annual commitments and actual outturns as different measures. Currency conversion, inflation adjustment and per-capita comparisons come only after scope and periods match. Funding is not a predictor in the current life-expectancy model: there is no comparable historical series or defensible causal lag yet.

The SG NMRC catalyst-award page could not be retrieved through either direct access or the extraction provider during this run. That failure is recorded locally and is not used to infer absence of funding.
