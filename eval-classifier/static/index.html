The deliverable is the measured agreement between the pipeline and human judgment — the target band is ≥ 85%, but an honest low number beats a lenient high one: disagreements are the product, they quantify classifier reliability, so judge each label strictly on the rubric. Read the title and quote (the exact text the classifier saw), then Agree if the label is right or Disagree and pick the correct label — ambiguous (on-topic but unplaceable) and not_relevant (not about human ageing at all) are first-class answers, never force a category. Rubric: eval-classifier/README.md and classifier/KEYWORDS.md (relevance gate first, then mechanism-vs-intervention). Where AI judge pre-marks (relevance, category, evidence) exist, flagged records sort first — the AI verdicts are advice only: your click is the verdict. Verdicts save to eval-classifier/verdicts/<you>.verdicts.json; several reviewers can work at once and the numbers merge. Add ?assign=1of2 / ?assign=2of2 to the URL to split the records deterministically between two reviewers.
