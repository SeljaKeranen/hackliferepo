A finding counts as correct only if all four checks pass: the source URL resolves, the source date is right, the classification is right, and the claim is supported by the source. Three AI judges (credibility, recency, classification) have pre-marked each finding — findings they flag as fail or uncertain are sorted first. The AI verdicts are advice only: your click is the verdict. Agree marks all four checks pass; Disagree asks which check fails; the per-check buttons override in detail. Verdicts save automatically to eval/verdicts/.
